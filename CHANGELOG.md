# Changelog

Versions are date stamps: `[year.month.day.time]`. Each script carries its own stamp on its first line.

## Widget (`widget-overengineered-solar.js`)

- **2026.10.28.1117** — The orange is a little darker, #c98a45 (was #d1a066), because the soft amber read too pale. Same places as before.
- **2026.10.28.0958** — Fixed: the Banked Credits number now follows your utility's bills. Setup asks for the bill day of the month. On each bill day a bank below zero goes back to zero (a positive bank stays), and on the bill day of the true-up month the credits left over are forfeited, so the bank goes to zero. A short month uses its last day. Without a saved bill day the day of your entered bill date is used. The 12-month bank line follows the same rules. The debug report has a new "Bank rule" line. The first run after updating fetches the daily history once more (3 requests). Needs setup 2026.10.28.0956 for the new field.
- **2026.10.28.0647** — A new layout on the medium widget, and a softer orange. The four boxes are now Prod 24h and 30d Prod vs Use on top, with Array Status and Banked Credits below, so Array Status moves to the bottom left. The Array Status heading now reads "Status:" followed by today's production so far, for example "Status: 101 kWh": green when you are ahead of yesterday at this time, orange when you are behind. When something is off, a word goes in front of the figure and the figure takes its colour: "Dirty" (orange, or red when the peak has dropped a lot), "Winter" (the sun is too low to judge) or "Service Required" (red, with no figure). The big number is Max kW again and is always green, so the colour sits only on the heading line. Orange is now a softer, sandier orange everywhere, on the medium and small widgets, so a low winter day reads as "a little behind" and not as a warning. When there is no power curve yet, the Array Status box shows an empty chart instead of a flat bar. The small and Lock Screen widgets are otherwise unchanged. The README and printable guide have new pictures and a new "Example days" section.
- **2026.10.19.1654** — Array Status: the "Max kW" number is now the highest of the last five clear-day peaks, not the second highest, so it matches the peak you see in the Enphase app. A day the weather service calls overcast now also counts when its peak is at least as high as the best saved peak, because a high peak can't be a false alarm. Found when a bright day with thin high cloud (23.9 kW in the Enphase app) was left out and the widget showed 23.2.
- **2026.10.16.0600** — Today view: the comparison with yesterday now also works when Enphase sends one reading short (95 instead of 96), which turned out to be common. The missing 15 minutes is filled with the reading beside it.
- **2026.10.16.0555** — Today view: when there is nothing to compare with yet (early each morning, until yesterday's totals are saved), Prod and Cons now show green instead of grey. Grey there wasn't obvious and only raised questions.
- **2026.10.16.0550** — Fixed: in the Today view the comparison with yesterday never worked between midnight and the first production fetch of the morning, because production isn't fetched overnight. It now compares at the actual time, so Prod and Cons get their colours as soon as yesterday's totals are saved (the first daily history fetch after 5 AM).
- **2026.10.16.0540** — Today view: when there is no comparison with yesterday yet (early each morning, until the first daily history fetch after 5 AM), Prod and Cons now show in the same grey the widget uses for every "no verdict", instead of near-white.
- **2026.10.15.1530** — Today view: the "ahead of yesterday at this time" colours now line up to the minute with yesterday. When Enphase sends one reading short and that can't be done, that update shows the numbers in neutral grey instead of a colour that could be 15 minutes off. The numbers themselves were never affected.
- **2026.10.15.1525** — On the Array Status chart, yesterday's part of the curve (left of the midnight dot) is faded, so today's part stands out. On the medium and small widgets.
- **2026.10.15.1520** — A Net of exactly 0 is now green on the medium widget too (it was grey), the same as on the small widget. A bank of exactly 0 is green as well.
- **2026.10.15.1515** — New: a Today view. Type `today` in a widget's Parameter box (Edit Widget) and its top-left numbers count from midnight instead of the past 24 hours, under the heading "Prod Today". Works on the medium and small sizes; leave the box empty for the past 24 hours, as before. In the Today view, Prod is green when it is ahead of yesterday at the same time and Cons is green when it is under yesterday at the same time (part of a day can't be judged against a whole day's average). The daily history now also saves yesterday's totals for this; the first run after updating fetches it once more (3 requests). The debug report has a new "Today view" line.
- **2026.10.15.1305** — A little more room (4 points instead of 2) between the Cons and Net numbers and their labels, on the medium and small widgets. Nothing else moves; checked with the widest numbers.
- **2026.10.15.1255** — The medium widget's top-left box now matches the small widget: the heading reads "Prod 24h", production is the big number (30 points, larger than the other three boxes), and Cons and Net sit beside it. Net moved there from the bank label line. The small trend mark beside "Banked Credits" is gone, because the 12-month bank chart already shows which way the bank is heading (the debug report still lists the 28-day trend). Checked with production up to 250 and Net from -110 to 210.
- **2026.10.15.1220** — New: a Small size. Add the same script as a Small widget, or resize the medium one while editing the Home Screen, and it draws a compact layout: the past 24 hours' production as one big number, Cons (or Exp and Imp) and Net beside it, and the Array Status box underneath. A title longer than 14 characters is cut back to the whole words that fit ("OverEngineered Solar" shows as "OverEngineered"). Same data as the medium widget, so the numbers match; no extra requests of its own. The medium and Lock Screen widgets are unchanged.
- **2026.10.14.1750** — The very faint vertical lines are gone: on the small boxes they crossed the charts like a crosshair. Small dots on the bottom edge of each chart give the sense of time instead, a little taller and stronger than the 1600 bank dots (2 points tall, 40%): the start of each quarter on the 12-month bank chart, the 1st of the month on the 30d box, and midnight on the Array Status chart. No extra requests.
- **2026.10.14.1600** — The 12-month bank chart gets four tiny, very faint dots on its bottom edge at the start of each quarter (Jan, Apr, Jul and Oct 1), so the line has a sense of time. Jan and Jul line up with the existing faint vertical lines. Switch off with `BANK_QUARTER_TICKS = false` in the script. No extra requests.
- **2026.10.14.1005** — The 30d box: the average-use line is now dashed, like the reference lines on the other two charts, and a very faint vertical line marks the 1st of the month. The line is left out when the 30 points are not 30 calendar days. No extra requests.
- **2026.10.14.0745** — The 30-day box heading now reads "30d Prod vs Use" (was "30-Day Avg Prod"), so it is clear the dotted line in the box is your average use. Nothing else changed.
- **2026.10.14.0700** — A tidier layout. 30-Day Avg Prod: the line is now framed in a box like the two charts below it, with "kWh/d" under the number. Banked Credits: "kWh" under the number when the bank chart is on. Array Status: the peak is set like the bank number (same size, "Max kW" under it), and when something is off the heading itself says so ("Array Dirty", "Array Very Dirty", "Winter Sun", "Service Required") in the status colour, replacing the label under the number. The worst states now use a lighter red-orange instead of a harsh red.
- **2026.10.14.0630** — Very faint vertical lines give the two small charts a sense of time, with no labels: Jan 1 and Jul 1 on the 12-month bank chart, and midnight on the Array Status chart (shown whether or not the bank chart is on). They read the same in every country.
- **2026.10.13.2245** — The 12-month bank chart is now on by default, also for settings files from before the switch existed (switch it off in setup for the original layout). Its line uses one point every two weeks instead of every week: at this size weekly points drew weather wobble rather than the shape, most of all on small arrays. The top of the box still comes from every day. With the chart on, the two small charts at the bottom now sit at exactly the same height.
- **2026.10.13.2230** — New, optional (setup switch "12-month bank chart"): the bank box can show a small line of the bank over the last 12 months, drawn like the Array Status curve. The top of the box is the highest balance in those 12 months and moves by itself as months roll by; the dotted line is today's balance. Today's Net moves up beside the trend arrow. The line is worked out from the daily history the widget already fetches, so it makes no extra requests: from your bill date on it is the same sum as the bank number, before the bill date it is worked back from the bill, and before a true-up it is counted up from zero. Months before your history begins are left blank. With the switch off, or too little history, the box looks exactly as before. The first run after updating fetches the daily history once more.
- **2026.10.13.2140** — Fixed two small errors in today's numbers. (1) The reading that ends exactly at midnight covers the last minutes of the day before, but was counted in today's consumption, so consumption since midnight (and Net) read a little off. (2) Production is only fetched while the sun is up, so the last readings before sunset were missing until the next morning and the evening totals ran a bit low. The widget now makes one extra production request about 30 minutes after sunset (one request a day) to pick them up. After dark, an old production reading now shows as 0 kW on the curve end instead of the last daytime value.
- **2026.10.12.1613** — Two runs a few minutes apart, too soon for a new reading, no longer count as "Enphase has nothing new", so they don't push back the next update.
- **2026.10.12.1609** — Fixed: when Enphase's cloud got stuck on one reading for hours, every wake of the widget looked due for new data, and it could use up its whole day of requests in about an hour, then freeze until midnight. Now each meter waits at least 45 minutes between fetches, and up to an hour while Enphase has nothing new, so a stalled feed costs no more than a normal day and the widget catches up within the hour once Enphase does. In daylight the time turns orange when the newest reading is more than 2 hours old, so old numbers never look current. The debug report now lists today's runs (Home Screen, Lock Screen, app) and which ones made requests, plus a line on how fresh Enphase's data is.
- **2026.10.08.1256** — Fixed: on the Lock Screen widget the time could still be cut off ("12:0...") when Net had a decimal, such as "+4.7 kWh". The numbers now step down a size a little sooner, and the time may shrink slightly to fit.
- **2026.10.07.1314** — The report you get by running the widget inside Scriptable is now called the debug report, the same name setup uses. Nothing in it changed.
- **2026.10.07.1241** — Fixed: on the Lock Screen widget the time could be cut off ("12:3..."). It is slightly smaller now.
- **2026.10.07.1236** — Fixed: on the Lock Screen widget a number could be cut off ("+..."), seen with a Net of "+12 kWh". The label column is narrower and the shared number size is now picked so the widest line fits.
- **2026.10.07.1022** — New: owners outside the US can enter a latitude and longitude in setup in place of a ZIP code. The widget then shows the weather icon and runs the dirty-panel check as it does with a ZIP. Not yet tested outside the US.
- **2026.10.07.1015** — Lock Screen widget: the three numbers are now always the same size, with smaller units beside them, and it asks iOS for a refresh every 15 minutes outside quiet hours so it keeps up with the Home Screen widget. Those refreshes make no Enphase requests. Changed: with no ZIP code saved, the widget no longer gives a Dirty or Very Dirty verdict, because without a location it cannot tell dirt from a low winter sun. The number shows as Max Power, and the status report says the check is off.
- **2026.10.07.0805** — New: the "Show Exported / Imported" setting now works. Switched on, the top left box shows energy sent to the grid and taken from it in place of Cons, worked out from 15-minute readings the widget already has. Off by default. Also added, switched off in the code: a small label for the newest power reading beside "Array Status".
- **2026.10.07.0710** — Comments in the code shortened. No change in behavior.
- **2026.10.07.0705** — The banked balance now counts overnight consumption as the night goes on; before, it held yesterday's closing figure until the first production fetch after sunrise. New: a small Lock Screen tile. Add the same script to the Lock Screen in the wide size and it shows three numbers counted from midnight: today's production, today's peak power and today's net, with the time of the newest reading and the weather icon beside them. It uses the numbers the home-screen widget already has, so it adds no Enphase requests. The status report says what the tile would show.
- **2026.10.06.1550** — If consumption cannot be fetched but production can, the fresh production is now shown with the last good consumption (time in orange) instead of both staying old.
- **2026.10.06.1310** — Net is now worked out from the Prod and Cons numbers as shown, so the three always agree.
- **2026.10.06.1225** — When the array is running near its peak, the icon is the bright sun even if the weather service says cloudy. The status report says which icon was shown and why.
- **2026.10.06.1215** — Sturdier when Enphase or the connection fails: a bad reply to the once-a-day history request is no longer saved, and the widget keeps showing its last good numbers (time in orange) instead of an error screen. The error screen is in plain language and says what to check. The status report opens with the widget version, time and phone, lists any problems first, and masks the system ID. The weather icon refreshes about every half hour in daylight at no cost in Enphase requests. Numbers under 10 show one decimal (the bottom-left box stays whole). New setting for utilities that do not bank kWh: the bottom-left box reads "Net Since" with your date. The report lists recent weather readings beside the array's power.
